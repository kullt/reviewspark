## 🛠️ CARRD BUILDING TIPS

### Layout Structure
1. **Hero** → Full-width, minimal, high impact
2. **Benefits** → 3-column grid (collapses to stack on mobile)
3. **How It Works** → Horizontal timeline (vertical on mobile)
4. **Email Form** → Contrasting background color for focus
5. **Testimonials** → Card layout with subtle shadows
6. **Pricing** → Side-by-side cards, highlight the Pro plan
7. **FAQ** → Clean stacked layout
8. **Final CTA** → Strong color contrast, repeat email form or scroll link

### Mobile Optimization
- Ensure text sizes scale down appropriately
- 3-column layouts become stacked single columns
- CTA buttons remain full-width and tappable
- Form fields are large enough for mobile typing

### Conversion Best Practices
- One clear CTA above the fold (hero)
- Email form repeated after social proof
- Urgency/scarcity in early access messaging
- Social proof placed before pricing
- Risk reversal ("free trial, no credit card")
